The hard part

A feed you can’t trust is worse than no feed.

Collecting metrics is a script anyone can write. Telling a real change apart from a rebuilt hash in a filename is the whole product. Every candidate event is confirmed by a second run, matched by stable resource identity and content hash, and collapsed across the pages it appears on before anyone sees it.
