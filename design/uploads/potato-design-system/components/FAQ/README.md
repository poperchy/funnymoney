# FAQ

`faq.accordion` — a `block` headline with a “Not here? Ask us directly →” line on the left; questions on the right as 17px rows between `line-strong` rules. The + disc (`accent` on `accent-soft`) rotates 45° and fills with `grad-primary` when open; one answer open at a time, the first by default.

- Questions are what people actually ask; answers are one to two sentences in 15/1.6 `ink-secondary`.
